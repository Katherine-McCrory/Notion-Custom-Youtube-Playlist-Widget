How to Use

⚠️ Critical steps (must be done after any and all settings changes):

  1. Open Settings ().
  2. Choose your custom Card Color, Accent Color, Opacity, and optional Background Image.
  3. Paste your YouTube Playlist Link into the field and click Load.
  4. Set or auto-generate a Unique ID for your character/player.
  5. Click "Copy Permanent Embed Link".
  6. Paste the link into Notion as an Embed to replace the current widget block.

!!You must follow these steps, and generate a new permanent link, every time you make any changes to your settings!! Without a new permanent link, your new settings will not be saved.
